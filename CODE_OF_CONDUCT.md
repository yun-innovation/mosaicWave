# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not acceptable.
- Keep issues on topic and free of other people's private information.
- Maintainers may edit, close or remove comments and issues that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

Report a problem privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
